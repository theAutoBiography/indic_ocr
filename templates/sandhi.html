How to Mark Sandhi Points
  • What is a sandhi point? - A sandhi point is where two words join together with phonetic changes
  • Where to click: Click on the vertical lines between characters where the sandhi split occurs
  • Example: In धृतराष्ट्र उवाच (dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca) → splits to धृतराष्ट्रः + उवाच, mark the split after 'र' and before 'उ'
  • Multiple splits: Some words have multiple sandhi points - mark all of them
  • Roman text: IITHLP transliteration is shown below the word to help identify phonemes
